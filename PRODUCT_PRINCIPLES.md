# Product Principles

## Learning comes first
English Level Up is a learning system with a game presentation layer. The educational model is the product; gamification is optional visual feedback.

- Learning scores, benchmarks, Current State, confidence, independence and transfer are evidence-based only.
- XP, coins, levels, streaks, missions and future cosmetics never change proficiency.
- The UI must surface learning priorities before cosmetic rewards.

## Visual direction
The product should feel like a restrained 64-bit / pixel-era game UI without becoming visually noisy.

- Use pixel-art motifs as accents: avatar, map, checkpoint, icons and small progress elements.
- Keep data surfaces clean, modern and highly readable.
- Prefer subtle borders, low-contrast backgrounds and generous spacing over heavy pixel frames.
- Avoid filling the screen with sprites, effects, gradients or competing reward elements.
- Game information should be glanceable and secondary to learning information.
- Body copy and analytics use normal readable typography; monospace is reserved for game labels, IDs and compact status UI.

## Daily mission
Daily Five is a consistency mechanic: five completed substantive sessions of at least 10 minutes on Monday through Friday. Weekends are optional bonus days. Shorter substantive sessions remain valid learning sessions but do not advance the mission.

## Currency
Coins are reserved for future cosmetics such as skins, avatars and world decoration. Cosmetics must never provide an academic advantage.
